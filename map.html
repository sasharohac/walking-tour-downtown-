Cheap eats: three meals under $10

A loop from the Hall Building to the three best-rated cheap spots nearby, in rating order: bagels, then shawarma, then a Lebanese bakery. About 800 m in all.

Ranked by rating

  1. Start

    Hall Building, 1455 De Maisonneuve W

    Leave by the main doors and turn right. Walk to Mackay and cross it.

  2. 1
    Montreal bagels

    Hinnawi Bros, 2002 Mackay

    Bagel and coffee shop, a few steps up Mackay from De Maisonneuve.

    Check: one bagel sandwich under $10
    4.4 ★RestoMontreal, 192 ratings
  3. 2
    Shawarma

    Boustan, 2020A Crescent

    Serving shawarma since 1986. Pierre Elliott Trudeau is said to have been a regular.

    Check: a pita under $10
    4.3 ★Google, 3,400+ reviews
  4. 3
    Lebanese bakery

    Al-Taib, at Guy and De Maisonneuve

    On Guy since November 1995. Known for Lebanese-style pizzas and pies, and cheap prices.

    Check: its exact door (listings say 2125 Guy) and a meal under $10
    4.0 ★Google, 1,400+ reviews
  5. End

    Back at the Hall Building

    Cross back over Guy and walk east on De Maisonneuve past Mackay. The Hall Building is on your left.

Ratings as listed in autumn 2026: Google figures via RestoMontreal (Boustan, Aug 2026; Al-Taib, Oct 2026). Hinnawi Bros' 4.4 is RestoMontreal's own score for its downtown café, not a Google rating. The top two are close, so check Google before you publish.
